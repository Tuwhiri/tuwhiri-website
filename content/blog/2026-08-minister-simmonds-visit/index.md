Hon Penny Simmonds, Minister of Science, Innovation and Technology and Minister for Tertiary Education, visited the Tuwhiri photonics laboratory at the University of Otago on 11 August 2026.

The visit follows one by her predecessor, Dr Shane Reti, in December 2025, which means both ministers holding the science portfolio during the programme's first year have now seen the instrument development first hand.

The timing was useful. The programme's kick-off meeting three weeks earlier had brought the atmospheric science, photonics and engineering teams together to settle what the instrument must achieve, so there was a clearer story to tell than there would have been in the first months of the programme.
