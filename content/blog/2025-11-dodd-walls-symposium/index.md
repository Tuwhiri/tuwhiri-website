Mallika Suresh took part in the Dodd-Walls Centre annual symposium, held from 24
to 27 November 2025.

The Dodd-Walls Centre is New Zealand's Centre of Research Excellence for
Photonic and Quantum Technologies, and one of Tuwhiri's affiliated partners. Its
annual symposium puts most of the country's photonics community in one room,
which makes it the natural place to present the up-conversion work and to meet
the students the programme will need over the next five years.
